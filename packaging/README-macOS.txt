SuperSaga - Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga, native for macOS

1. Drag SuperSaga into the Applications folder.
2. Open SuperSaga. The first time, macOS may say it is from an unidentified developer:
   right-click (or Control-click) SuperSaga, choose Open, then Open again.
3. Click "Choose ROM..." and pick your own dump of Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga (USA).
   SuperSaga checks the file and remembers where it is. It never copies or uploads it.
4. Press Play. "Create Shortcut" adds a launcher that starts the game directly.

Settings and saves are kept in:
  ~/Library/Application Support/SuperSaga/SuperSaga/
